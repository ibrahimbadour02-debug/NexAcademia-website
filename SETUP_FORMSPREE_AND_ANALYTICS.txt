NEXACADEMIA — FORMSPREE ENQUIRY + GOOGLE ANALYTICS SETUP

The website design and other features have been left unchanged.
Only the enquiry delivery method has been switched from Netlify Forms to Formspree.

PART A — MAKE ENQUIRIES ARRIVE IN YOUR EMAIL

1. Go to https://formspree.io and create a free account.
2. Create a new form/project for NexAcademia.
3. Formspree will give you a form endpoint similar to:
   https://formspree.io/f/abcxyzde

4. Copy ONLY the final form ID:
   abcxyzde

5. In your GitHub NexAcademia repository, open:
   assets/content.js

6. Find:
   formspreeFormId:""

7. Put your Formspree form ID between the quotation marks, for example:
   formspreeFormId:"abcxyzde"

8. Commit the change in GitHub.
9. Netlify will automatically redeploy the website.
10. Open the LIVE website and submit a test enquiry.
11. Confirm the submission in your Formspree dashboard and check the email inbox
    connected to your Formspree account.

The website will:
- send the existing enquiry fields to Formspree,
- show a Sending... state while submitting,
- display an error if submission fails,
- redirect successful enquiries to thank-you.html.

PART B — GOOGLE ANALYTICS 4

No change has been made to the analytics setup.

1. Create a Google Analytics 4 property and Web data stream.
2. Copy your Measurement ID, which looks like:
   G-XXXXXXXXXX
3. In assets/content.js find:
   gaMeasurementId:""
4. Replace it with your ID:
   gaMeasurementId:"G-XXXXXXXXXX"
5. Commit the change.
6. Netlify will redeploy automatically.
7. Check Google Analytics Realtime after visiting the live site.

EXISTING ANALYTICS EVENTS KEPT
- open_enquiry
- click_phone
- select_pathway
- support_finder_complete
- student_challenge_correct
- enquiry_submit_attempt
- generate_lead

The generate_lead event now identifies the successful enquiry method as Formspree.

IMPORTANT
Do not paste your personal receiving email address into the website code.
Formspree manages the destination email from your Formspree account/dashboard.
